# Soil Health

Soil health describes the continued capacity of soil to function as a living ecosystem that supports plants, animals, microorganisms and important ecosystem processes.

## Soil Organic Carbon

Soil organic carbon is an important indicator of soil condition. Organic carbon is associated with soil structure, biological activity, nutrient cycling and other soil processes.

Very low soil organic carbon can indicate concerns about soil condition and may be associated with reduced biological activity and degradation risk. Soil organic matter can be supported through practices that reduce degradation and maintain biological activity.

## Soil Biodiversity

Soil biodiversity includes the diversity of organisms living in soil, including microorganisms and soil animals.

Soil organisms contribute to decomposition, nutrient processes, water filtration, carbon-related processes and agricultural production. Protecting soil biodiversity therefore supports important soil and ecosystem functions.

## Soil Health and Biodiversity Connection

Soil health and soil biodiversity are interconnected.

Changes in soil condition can affect the organisms and biological processes present in soil. Management practices that maintain soil organic matter, reduce degradation and protect biological activity can support both soil health and soil biodiversity.

## Monitoring Indicators

Useful indicators for environmental assessment can include:

- Soil organic carbon
- Soil moisture
- Biological activity
- Soil biodiversity
- Indicators of soil degradation

These indicators should be interpreted together with local climate, land-use conditions and management practices.

# Soil Health and Soil Biodiversity

## Soil biodiversity

Soil biodiversity includes the diversity of organisms living belowground, from genes and species to communities and ecological complexes.

Soil organisms contribute to important soil ecosystem services including:
- water filtration
- carbon sequestration
- decomposition
- agricultural production
- nutrient-related ecosystem processes

Loss of soil biodiversity is considered a major threat to soils in many regions.

## Management implication

Sustainable soil management should aim to prevent or minimize soil degradation and soil biodiversity loss.

Important monitoring concepts include:
- soil organic carbon
- soil moisture
- soil biological activity
- soil biodiversity
- indicators of soil degradation

## Relevance to Darukaa.Earth

When soil condition is poor, recommendations should consider both soil-health metrics and biodiversity outcomes rather than treating soil as an isolated variable.
